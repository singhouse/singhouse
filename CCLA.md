# Singhouse Corporate Contributor License Agreement

**Version 1.0**

> **Prepared for publication; CLA intake and automated enforcement are not yet active.**

## When to use this agreement

Use this agreement when contributions to Singhouse are made by employees, contractors, or agents in the course of their work for an organization, so that the organization — which usually owns the resulting intellectual property — grants the necessary rights directly.

An organization signs this agreement once. Individuals contributing on its behalf are listed in **Schedule A** and do not need to sign the Individual CLA for work covered here. Anyone contributing on their own time, outside the scope of their employment, should sign the Individual CLA instead.

Singhouse is published under the **AGPL-3.0**. Bones Consulting LLC also develops **premium commercial add-ons** and offers commercial licences. This agreement gives Us the rights needed to do both. It is a licence, not an assignment: the Corporation keeps ownership of its code.
