Why it's here: exported CD+G graphics are rasterised server-side, so the
renderer needs a font that ships with the package rather than whatever the
host happens to have installed. DejaVu Sans Bold stays legible at the small
pixel sizes a 300x216 screen allows and covers Latin, Cyrillic, and Greek
with wide punctuation coverage, so most lyric text renders without
substitution boxes.
