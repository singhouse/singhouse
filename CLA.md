## Why this agreement exists

Singhouse is released to the public under the **GNU Affero General Public License, version 3.0 (AGPL-3.0)**. Alongside that open source core, Bones Consulting LLC develops and sells **premium commercial add-ons** and offers **commercial licenses** to organizations that cannot or do not wish to comply with the AGPL-3.0.

The AGPL-3.0 alone does not give us the rights we need to do that with code we did not write. If a contribution reached us under AGPL-3.0 terms only, we could not lawfully include it in a proprietary add-on or grant a customer a commercial license covering it. This agreement gives us those rights.

**What this means for you:**

- You keep the copyright in your contribution. This is a licence, not an assignment. You can reuse, relicense, or sell your own code however you like.
- We can license your contribution under AGPL-3.0 *and* under commercial terms, including as part of paid, closed-source add-ons.
- We commit in Section 4 that anything we merge into the open source core stays available under a free and open source licence, permanently.
- We do not owe you money if we make money. If that is not acceptable to you, please do not sign — no hard feelings, and you are welcome to fork under the AGPL.

By signing this agreement, or by submitting a contribution after indicating acceptance through the process described in Section 13, you agree to the terms below.
